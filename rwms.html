CASE 01 · PRODUCT & OPERATIONS Avionic Consulting Solution • May – July 2026

Recruitment Workflow Management System (RWMS)

Consolidating 9–10 fragmented spreadsheets into an integrated AppSheet operational CRM for 12+ recruiters and 10+ corporate client accounts.

THE OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

Recruitment operations were scattered across 9–10 disconnected Excel workbooks on recruiters' local laptops. This caused missed candidate follow-ups, duplicate submissions, and forced the Founder to spend ~90 minutes every evening manually compiling status reports.

THE ARCHITECTED SOLUTION

Designed an AppSheet operational CRM with role-based permissions, automated Google Apps Script email notifications, stage-gate recruiter validations, and real-time SLA metrics that consolidated all operations into one centralized database.

[ 01 · OPERATIONAL CONTEXT ]

The Root Cause: Siloed Information Architecture

Avionic Consulting Solution handles 20+ active enterprise clients and 150–200 monthly candidates. Without a shared MIS, operations suffered from 4 systemic failure modes:

BOTTLENECK 01

Missed Candidate Follow-Ups

Follow-up dates lived in individual personal spreadsheets, leading to communication drops and candidate drop-offs during client interview rounds.

BOTTLENECK 02

Duplicate Candidate Outreach

Without unique identity validation, recruiters independently contacted the same candidate for identical client requisitions, creating client embarrassment.

BOTTLENECK 03

Heavy Founder Reporting Overhead

The Founder spent ~1.5 hours daily chasing updates across recruiters and manually formatting status digests for corporate client leads.

BOTTLENECK 04

Opaque Recruiter Throughput

Leadership lacked visibility into who was actively sourcing versus stalled, preventing timely resource reallocations on urgent positions.

[ ISHIKAWA ROOT CAUSE FRAMEWORK ] Operational Breakdown
PEOPLE

12+ recruiters operating in informational silos with differing spreadsheet formatting habits.

PROCESS

No standardized stage-gate exit criteria between Sourced, Screened, and Client-Submitted.

TOOLS

GOVERNANCE

No real-time SLA breach alerts or centralized client status reporting dashboard.

[ 03 · ITERATION MATRIX ]

System Design & The 15+ User Feedback Sprints

Rather than purchasing a rigid off-the-shelf ATS with high seat fees, we architected a bespoke, low-code operational CRM directly around Avionic's consulting delivery models.

FRONTEND UI
AppSheet Mobile/Web

Role-based views for Recruiters vs. Founder with stage-gate data rules.

LOGIC ENGINE
Google Apps Script

Automated email triggers, duplicate email detection, and SLA flags.

DATABASE
Relational Cloud Sheets

Primary keys, relational foreign tables for Candidates, Jobs, & Clients.

ANALYTICS
Power BI Executive MIS

Real-time throughput, SLA fulfillment, and recruiter leaderboard.

THE V1 → V2 PRODUCT EVOLUTION 15+ User Feedback Sprints
OPERATIONAL MODULE WHAT BROKE IN V1 HOW V2 SOLVED IT
Candidate Intake Recruiters complained 14 mandatory fields took >4 mins per candidate. Streamlined to 5 initial fields; progressive disclosure unlocks remaining fields at interview stage.
Duplicate Detection Only checked exact full-name matches, missing candidate entries with spelling variations. Implemented Phone Number + Normalized Email hashing with instant in-form warning flags.
Daily Status Reports Founder still had to manually export CSVs to format client update emails. Automated a daily 7:00 PM HTML client summary email trigger sent directly to client account managers.
SLA Stage Gates Candidates got stuck in "Screened" without accountability. Added 48-hour automated amber alerts and 72-hour red escalation tags on recruiter dashboards.
[ 04 · EXECUTIVE TAKEAWAYS ]

What Building RWMS Taught Me

01 / ADOPTION > CODE

The toughest part of internal software isn't the data model — it's driving behavioral adoption. Shadowing recruiters, mapping their pain points, and removing needless friction was what turned spreadsheet resistance into 100% daily buy-in.

02 / RAPID ITERATIONS

V1 is never the final product. Shipping a functional MVP within 7 days and running 15+ rapid micro-feedback sprints was 10x more effective than attempting to architect a perfect system in a vacuum.

03 / QUANTIFY ROI

Every operational feature must justify itself in business terms. Reclaiming ~1.5 hours daily for the Founder and eliminating client SLA breaches translated directly into agency revenue growth and client contract renewals.
